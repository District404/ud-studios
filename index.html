SILENT KILL

The world knows his name. Almost no one understands who they're dealing with.

Slur is a respected CEO, a man of immense wealth and influence, and one of the world's deadliest assassins. These aren't two sides of him that take turns. They're both part of who he is. He operates in both worlds with the same authority, keeping his private life protected while his reputation continues to grow.

His bounty begins at one billion dollars. Every powerful enemy he eliminates drives the price higher, pushing it toward an unimaginable one trillion dollars. As the reward grows, increasingly dangerous hunters enter the game, all believing the money is worth the risk.

Behind his wealth and reputation lies a family he refuses to expose to his world. His wife and twelve-year-old daughter know him as a successful businessman, unaware of his life as an assassin. Only his nephew knows the truth. Trained by Slur himself, he remains inexperienced and has much to learn about the world his uncle navigates.

To the public, Slur is a successful CEO. To those hunting him, he's the most valuable target alive.

Every kill raises the bounty. Every hunter risks becoming the hunted.

And as the price on his head approaches one trillion dollars, the question isn't who wants him dead. It's who can afford to try.
